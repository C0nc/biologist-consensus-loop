# Decision log

One entry per decision, appended in order. This is the skeleton of the final report.
Never delete or rewrite an entry's text. A correction (the decision stands) gets an `Amendment` line under the entry. A reversal gets a new entry with `Supersedes: Dk`, and the old one gets `Superseded by: Dm`. Those are the only additions allowed to an old entry.
